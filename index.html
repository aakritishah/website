Bio

Hi! I'm a graduate student working on machine learning for healthcare and biomedical data. I recently completed my MS in Computer Science at USC and am finishing an MS in Computational Biomedicine & Biotechnology at the University of Pittsburgh (expected May 2027). I hold a BA in Computer Science and Business Management (Honors) from Rollins College.

My research spans several directions, but they share one goal: approaching technical systems with attention to the people they affect. In healthcare AI, that means building tools for medical imaging and digital pathology (prostate cancer segmentation and grading) and, more recently, genomic and sequence data, such as using protein language models to study viral evolution and antibody neutralization. In privacy and trustworthy ML, it means studying how unlearning in LLMs can protect sensitive information. And in human-centered AI, including my work this past summer at Northumbria University, it means designing with accessibility, identity, and community in mind.

Research

  • Barton Lab, University of Pittsburgh · Sept. 2026 – present Protein Language Models for HIV Antibody NeutralizationNew
    Testing whether ESM-2 embeddings of HIV-1 Env sequences improve a published antibody-neutralization predictor over its PCA features, on a CATNAP matrix of 806 antibodies × ~1,150 viruses. Reproduced the original model (Pearson R = 0.74) and built a paired evaluation with hold-outs for unseen viruses and subtypes, plus a random-projection control.
  • AIMed Lab · May 2025 – present Digital Pathology: Prostate Cancer
    Prostate cancer detection through tissue compartment and Gleason pattern segmentation in H&E whole slide images, using UNet, nnUNet, and the UNI foundation model. A new postprocessing step that reincorporates lumen brings the average Dice score to 0.895. Now extending to Gleason pattern grading (patterns 3, 4, 5) to assess cancer aggressiveness.
    Gleason Poster Tissue Poster
  • Diabetic Retinopathy Detection
    Preprocessing (CLAHE, Gaussian blur, MGA-CSG) to improve retinal fundus image quality, targeting poor dataset quality for earlier detection and more robust models.
  • USC × Defense Health Agency · Jan. – May 2025 AI for Battlefield TBI Care
    Project manager and developer on a student team building a three-part system with a Walter Reed clinician: an AI triage app, a drone resupply optimizer, and a portable eddy current hemorrhage detector, aimed at reducing preventable traumatic brain injury deaths.
  • ARKAI Lab · May 2025 – present Unlearning in LLMs
    Targeted unlearning as a privacy-preserving mechanism, using Harry Potter–unlearned Llama-2-7b models. Examines how knowledge entanglement shapes what leaks after unlearning, and tests resistance to adversarial extraction and membership inference. Submitted to EACL 2026.
    Paper Project
  • NSF IRES, Northumbria University (UK) · May – July 2026 Citizen-Centered AI
    Human-centered AI research on identity, privacy, security, and information, in an international and interdisciplinary team of US and UK researchers.
  • USC · Aug. – Dec. 2025 Unlearning Political Information in LLMs
    Applied unlearning methods (DPO, refusal tuning, prompting) to Llama-3.1-8B and Mistral-7B, then measured shifts in "political orientation" with political compass and social psychology questionnaires.
  • Conversational Hinglish Chatbot
    Fine-tuned LLMs for open-domain dialogue using multilingual and synthetic datasets; curated code-switched data, applied instruction-tuning, and evaluated syntactic and semantic quality.
    Paper Project
  • Honors Thesis, Rollins College · 2023 Evaluating AI Sentiment Analysis
    Compared human and LLM sentiment analysis on nonprofit interview data, exploring LLM limits in low-resource and emotionally nuanced settings.
    Paper Project
  • NSF REU, Penn State · Summer 2023 Adversarial Attacks in Autonomous Driving Systems
    Investigated adversarial robustness of ML models for autonomous driving, analyzing transfer learning and robustness across architectures.
    Paper
